California Required Workplace Training Overview

California requires several mandatory workplace training programs to ensure safety, prevent harassment, and maintain compliance with state labor laws.

Mandatory Training Programs

  • Sexual Harassment & Abusive Conduct Prevention (SB 1343)
  • Workplace Violence Prevention (SB 553)
  • Injury & Illness Prevention Program (IIPP)
  • Heat Illness Prevention
  • Hazard Communication (HazCom)

Who Must Complete These Trainings?

  • Supervisors & Managers
  • Non‑supervisory Employees
  • Temporary & Seasonal Workers
  • Outdoor Workers
